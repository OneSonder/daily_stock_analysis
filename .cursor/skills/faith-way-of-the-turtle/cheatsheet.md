# Cheatsheet — Way of the Turtle

## Decision rules
- If you cannot state markets + size + entry + stop + exit + tactics → **not a complete system** — don't trade it.
- If expectancy is unknown/unpositive after costs → **do not size up**.
- If recent losers tempt you to skip a market → **take the signal anyway** (recency trap; cocoa lesson).
- If short-term pain after breakout entry → **expected** for trend entries; don't confuse with broken edge.
- If drawdown approaches what you "thought you could handle" → you already oversized — **cut risk for next cycle**, don't selectively pick trades mid-DD.
- If a backtest looks miraculous → assume **trader effects, luck, optimization, or overfitting** until proven otherwise.
- If two systems rank differently across quiet vs volatile trends → **don't crown a single champion**; diversify systems.
- If account is too small for volatile markets at safe % risk → **trade zero contracts** there.
- If ego wants a prediction or a secret → switch to **probabilities + written rules**.

## Risk sizing defaults (Turtle-inspired)
| Item | Rule of thumb |
|------|----------------|
| Unit risk | 1N ≈ 1% equity (classic); Faith often suggests more conservative early |
| Stop | Often ~2N from entry |
| Adds | Every ~½N favorable, up to caps |
| Caps | 4 / market · 6 correlated · 10 loose · 12 direction |
| Personal DD buffer | Target sim DD ≤ ~½ psychological max |
| Early-career returns | Prefer steady ~20–30% compounding over 100%+ gamble |

## System choice tree
- Need large moves, can stand ~65–70% losers → **trend following**
- Short-horizon fades near S/R, avoid strong trends → **countertrend**
- Multi-day swings → **swing**
- Flat daily → **day trading** (position/scalp/arb)
- Unknown future regime mix → **simple + diversified portfolio**, not max-MAR Omega

## Metric tells
| Prefer | Over |
|--------|------|
| RAR%, R-cubed, robust Sharpe | Raw CAGR%, MAR, Sharpe on one window |
| Multi-decade multi-state samples | Last 6–12 months paper trades |
| Process adherence stats | Last 10 trade P&L |

## Smell tests (you're in trouble)
- Moving stops away from pain
- Skipping signals after a market "felt cursed"
- Doubling size because "I'm due"
- Buying a vendor curve with "no losing months"
- Needing to be right about tomorrow's direction
