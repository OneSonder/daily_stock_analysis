# Glossary — Way of the Turtle

**Anchoring** — Over-relying on a salient reference price (often a recent high) when deciding (Ch 2).

**ATR (Average True Range)** — Volatility measure from true ranges; basis of Turtle **N** (Ch 8, 15).

**Bandwagon / herd effect** — Believing because many others believe; fuels late bubbles (Ch 2).

**Breakout** — Price exceeding N-day high/low; core Turtle entry building block (Ch 5, 9, 15).

**Cognitive bias** — Systematic distortion in judgment under uncertainty (Ch 2).

**Complete trading system** — Rules covering markets, sizing, entries, stops, exits, tactics (Ch 15).

**Countertrend trading** — Fade extremes / failed breakouts when markets mean-revert (Ch 2, 6).

**Curve fitting / overfitting** — System too tuned to history to generalize (Ch 11).

**Day trading** — Flat by close; may use position, scalp, or arbitrage styles (Ch 2).

**Disposition effect** — Cut winners, ride losers (Ch 2).

**Donchian channel** — Highest high / lowest low over N days (Ch 5, 10, 15).

**Drawdown** — Peak-to-trough equity decline; depth and duration matter (Ch 2, 7).

**Edge** — Exploitable statistical advantage likely to recur after costs (Ch 5).

**E-ratio (Edge Ratio)** — Volatility-normalized MFE/MAE after entry over n days (Ch 5).

**Expectation** — Long-run average outcome per unit risk; not win rate (Ch 3).

**Hedger** — Transfers business price risk into futures (Ch 1).

**Law of small numbers** — Over-inferring from tiny samples (Ch 2, 11).

**Liquidity risk** — Cannot transact at a fair price when needed (Ch 1).

**Loss aversion** — Prefer avoiding losses over acquiring equal gains (Ch 2).

**MAE / MFE** — Maximum adverse / favorable excursion after entry (Ch 5).

**MAR ratio** — Return/drawdown style ratio; endpoint-sensitive (Ch 7, 12).

**Market state** — Quiet/volatile × stable/trending regimes (Ch 2, 9, 13).

**Money management** — Choosing size and aggregate exposure to survive (Ch 8).

**N** — Turtle volatility unit ≈ 20-day ATR of true range (Ch 8, 15).

**Optimization paradox** — Parameter picking reduces out-of-sample trust in the same backtest (Ch 11).

**Outcome bias** — Judging decisions by results, not decision quality (Ch 2, 4).

**Portfolio / trend filter** — Allow trades only with long-term trend alignment (Ch 5, 10, 13).

**Price risk** — Risk of large adverse price moves (Ch 1).

**Price shock** — Sudden catastrophic move; stops may not save you (Ch 7, 8).

**Pyramiding** — Adding units on favorable ½N progress (Ch 15).

**RAR%** — Regressed annual return; robust slope of equity curve (Ch 12).

**R-cubed (RRRR)** — Robust risk/reward using RAR% and length-adjusted avg max DD (Ch 12).

**Recency bias** — Overweighting recent trades/data (Ch 2, 4).

**Risk of ruin** — Chance of losing enough to stop trading (Ch 3, 8).

**Robustness** — Performance holds across unknown futures; diversity + simplicity (Ch 12–13).

**Scalper / market maker** — Earns bid–ask spread; liquidity provider (Ch 1).

**Speculator / position trader** — Assumes price risk for directional profit (Ch 1).

**Sunk cost effect** — Letting already-lost money dictate holding losers (Ch 2).

**Support / resistance** — Behavioral price zones from prior extremes (Ch 6).

**Swing trading** — Short-hold directional trades (days) (Ch 2).

**System death** — Edge stops working after market dynamics change (Ch 7, 11).

**System 1 / System 2** — Turtle 20-day (with skip) and 55-day breakout entry systems (Ch 15).

**Trader effect** — Trading a pattern changes/crowds the pattern (Ch 11).

**Trend following** — Capture large multi-month moves; many small losers (Ch 2).

**True range** — max(H−L, H−prior close, prior close−L) (Ch 15).

**Unit** — Volatility-normalized position chunk; 1N ≈ 1% equity in classic Turtle sizing (Ch 8, 15).

**Unit limits** — Caps: 4/market, 6 correlated, 10 loose, 12 direction (Ch 8, 15).

**Volatility channel** — MA ± k·ATR or stdev band for entries (Ch 9–10).
