# Chapter 14: Mastering Your Demons (+ Closing Path)

## Core Idea
The market won't soothe your ego. Success requires facing truths about markets and about your limitations — then executing simple rules with consistency.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Ego vs systematic trading**: Discretion feeds ego ("I called it"); systems constrain ego.
- **Secrets demon**: Belief that profitability must hide complexity/secrets — blocks following simple taught rules.
- **Humble pie**: Humility enables probabilistic thinking and rule adherence.
- **Consistency as X-factor**: Faith credits Turtle success partly to confidence from being taught by Dennis — find equivalent faith via your own rigorous research.
- **Style–personality fit**: Trend following isn't for everyone; match temperament to style.

## Key Concepts
- **Live by the ego, die by the ego**: Aggressive bragging traders often blow up.
- **Ping-Pong tournament lesson**: Opponent with more skill lost under ego pressure; same pattern showed in his Turtle results (blamed missing "secrets").
- **Don't be an ass**: Status games among traders poison learning and culture.
- **Learning requires failure**: Path changes through honest feedback, not narrative protection.
- **On money**: Keep money as tool/score, not identity.

## Mental Models
- Prefer simple known truths over secret knowledge craving.
- Build confidence by simulating and stress-testing yourself — not by guru mythology alone.
- Seek psychology resources (Tharp, Steenbarger, Kiev, Douglas) if consistency is hard.

## Anti-patterns
- Ignoring rules because "it can't be that simple."
- Trading for forum glory / identity.
- Blaming incomplete secrets instead of execution failures.
- Forcing a style that conflicts with your psychology.

## Worked Example
Tournament: Faith plays defensive sandpaper style; superior player cracks under need to prove dominance. Parallel: that Turtle underperformed mechanically because ego blocked seeing internal causes. Separately, Faith catches himself bragging about max units to under-positioned peers — vows not to be "an ass."

## Key Takeaways
1. Ego is the enemy of expectancy.
2. Simplicity is a feature; secrets-hunger is a bug.
3. Consistency is the scarce skill.
4. Match style to personality.
5. Humility keeps you in the game.
